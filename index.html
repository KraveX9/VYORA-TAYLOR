Every client gets a permanent profile on Vyora. Name, phone, style preferences, order history, payment history — all in one place. Searchable forever.

Order Tracking

Problem: "Is my cloth ready?" — 20 times a day.

Every order moves through clear stages: Cutting → Sewing → Fitting → Finishing → Ready → Delivered. You always know where every job is. Your client always knows too.

Assign orders to staff, upload photos at each stage, and let Vyora update clients automatically. You get your time back.

  • Six clear order stages
  • Auto-updates client at each stage
  • Deadline tracking + overdue alerts
  • Assign to staff members

Invoices & Payment Recording

Problem: You write invoices on scraps of paper.

Generate a professional invoice in 30 seconds. Track deposits. Track balances. Record every payment. Your client gets a real invoice — not a handwritten note.

Vyora never handles the money. Your client pays you directly — bank transfer, cash, POS, whatever you prefer. You just record it in Vyora so you always know exactly what's been paid and what's still owed.

  • Professional invoice in 30 seconds
  • Track deposits and balances

  • Full payment history per client
FEATURE 07

Fabric Inventory

Problem: You don't know what fabric you have.

Track every roll of fabric — type, colour, quantity, cost. Vyora alerts you when stock is running low, and shows you which fabric hasn't moved in months.

Stop tying up money in fabric you're not using. Stop running out of the fabric you need. Know the true value of your inventory at all times.

  • Track fabric by type, colour, quantity
  • Low-stock alerts
  • Dead stock report
  • Total inventory value in real time
FEATURE 08

Staff & Shift Management

Problem: You can't watch every sale.

Add your staff, assign roles, set 4-digit PINs. Every sale is logged with the staff member who made it. Every shift is tracked with expected cash vs. actual cash.

Discrepancies are flagged instantly. You know exactly who sold what, when, and for how much — even when you're not there.

  • Add unlimited staff members
  • Track sales by staff member
  • Shift reconciliation with cash counts
  • Catch discrepancies instantly

Manual Measurement Vault

Problem: Measurements get lost, copied wrong, or forgotten.

Store 60+ body measurements for women, 50+ for men. Every measurement can be backed by a photo showing exactly where it was taken. Versioned — new sessions don't overwrite old ones.

When a client returns, their measurements are already there. No more re-measuring. No more disputes about whether the waist or hip was correct.

  • 60+ measurement points
  • Photo proof for each measurement
  • Versioned history (never overwritten)
  • Searchable forever

Do I need to download an app?

No. Vyora works in your browser. You can also install it to your home screen like an app — on iPhone, Android, or any device. It takes 5 seconds.

No. Vyora never touches your money. Your clients pay you directly — cash, bank transfer, or POS. You just record the payment in Vyora so you always know what's been paid and what's still owed.

Do I need to be tech-savvy?

No. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use Vyora. It's designed to be simple. We'll help you set it up personally when you start.

After 14 days, you subscribe at ₦2,500/month (Solo) or ₦5,000/month (Business). No automatic charges. You choose when to pay. If you don't subscribe, your account stays but the paid features lock.

Yes. The Business and Fashion House tiers let you add staff with their own PINs and roles. Each staff member sees only what they need to see. You see everything.

Does my website work on Google?

Yes. Every Vyora page is indexed by Google. When someone searches "tailor in [your city]," your page can appear in the results. This brings you new clients without you doing anything.

Can I use my own domain name?

Yes — on the Business and Fashion House tiers. You can use yourname.com instead of vyora.ng/yourname. We handle the technical setup for you.

What if I don't like it?
